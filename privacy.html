Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Pure Finish Cleaning ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Service address / ZIP code
  • Details about the service you're requesting

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to quote requests and schedule services
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, and scheduling updates via text message, email, or phone
  • To improve our services and customer communication

Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and opt in, we may send you text messages related to your quote, scheduling, and appointments. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP. Consent to receive texts is never a condition of purchase.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties except as needed to operate our business (e.g., scheduling tools, payment processors, SMS delivery providers) or as required by law.

Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as needed to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may request that we delete your information, opt out of text or email communications, or ask what information we have on file by contacting us using the details below.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website or the phone number provided in our communications with you.
